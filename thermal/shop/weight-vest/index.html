What you get

This weight vest is an essential piece of kit for bodyweight workouts and generally putting more weight onto your body for working out. You can load it up to some proper weight, up to 80kg.

It’s so good because you can take off the little discs and adjust the weight to whatever you need. It’s great for taking to the gym because you don’t have to lug the weights around: you can use the ones from the gym. At home you can get the weights you need and have your own set.

It holds real tight around your body with an adjustable fit and the position of the straps means you can go upside down without it slipping. It’s definitely a piece of kit you need in your bag.
