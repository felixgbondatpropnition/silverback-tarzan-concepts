The film · feature documentary, 94 minutes

You Can’t Fly

Are you ready to get cosmic with Silverback Tarzan?

Winner, Brighton Spirit AwardBrighton Rocks International Film Festival 2026

You Can’t Fly enters the vivid world and complex mind of Silverback Tarzan, a hang gliding sensation who lives in Brighton, England. An intimate portrait of resilience and hope, a love letter to life itself, You Can’t Fly shows us that whoever you are, wherever you come from, whatever has happened to you, there are always specks of light.

Follows Matt Lewis, aka Silverback Tarzan, as he comes to terms with being grounded following a near fatal hang-gliding accident. With the support of his partner Tara, his positivity wins through.
