Cosmic Retreat Day

£200

Experience a day with Silverback Tarzan. Deeper, in-person days to step out of routine and work on things together. We’ll take the day as it comes and work round what we’d like to do.

  • Training in the Silverback Garden

    Heat up, grow strength and verbalise to create your world. Music, breathing, focus and playfulness. Bring out your animal self.

  • Music

    Play some tunes with SBT in the cosmic den.

  • The King’s Feast

    Meat, fish or eggs, porridge, loads of colours and live food. Join in with the Silverback’s food for a day. Dietary requirements taken.

  • Chats

    Q&A, talk things through and generally build connection and exchanges.

When
Dates are flexible: we work it out when it fits for you and for us. Good availability, and the ticket is valid for a year.

Ticket to Fly

£180 plus £30 BHPA insurance on the day

Your first training day: Day 1 of the BHPA Elementary Pilot course.

Book at Airsports Sussex The full course
What you get
Theory, rigging and your first tethered flights, learning with Matt.
Where
Different spots around the South Downs National Park, depending on the weather.
Paying
Pay in three instalments of £60 if you like. Valid for a year.
When
Training normally runs March to October, and the flying call is made the evening before.
Who
Matt owns Airsports Sussex and is its Chief Flying Instructor. Fully registered and licensed with the BHPA (British Hang Gliding and Paragliding Association).

Tara Bentall

A lighter way to move

Helping people live with less pain, tension and stress. 1:1 and workshop-based work focused on awareness, how we respond, and lasting change.

Tara is Matt’s partner; together they run Silverback Tarzan. Individual work focused on movement, the nervous system and lasting change, rooted in the Alexander Technique. One-to-one sessions in her Portslade studio, for Brighton, Hove and Shoreham.
