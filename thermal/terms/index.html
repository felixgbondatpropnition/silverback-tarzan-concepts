Exchange any physical product you are not happy with, for whatever reason. If you are not happy with a Silverback Tarzan product, you’re welcome to exchange it or ask for a full refund. Just return the product within 2 weeks with your receipt. Patreon is non-refundable.

Undamaged goods

Where you have cancelled an order and the products have already been delivered to you please return them to us as soon as possible.

Where the products have already been delivered to you, we will process the refund due to you as soon as possible and within 14 days after the day on which we receive the returned products, or (if earlier) within 14 calendar days after the day you provide us with evidence that the products were returned to us.

If you need to return them to us you will be offered an exchange or a full refund, after we have received the returned products.
