Where
Different spots around the South Downs National Park, depending on the weather.
Paying
Pay in three instalments of £60 if you like. Valid for a year.

Training normally runs March to October, and the flying call is made the evening before.

Awareness and Response Practice

Tara Bentall

A lighter way to move.

1:1 and workshop-based work focused on awareness, how we respond, and lasting change.

Tara is Matt’s partner; together they run Silverback Tarzan. Her practice is individual work focused on movement, the nervous system and lasting change, rooted in the Alexander Technique. One-to-one sessions in her Portslade studio, for Brighton, Hove and Shoreham.
